Why Kemiri

A little help. A bigger picture.

Without a harness, every one of these is a habit you or your agents must remember.

  • Kept by handWith Kemiri
  • A tab for every agent, each with its own transcript
    Separate chats, one projectGive a coordinator a goal. It delegates the pieces to background agents, on models from your own providers.
  • You keep checking whether an agent is stuck
    Questions and reviews come to youFind quick replies and open questions in Answers. Choose what interrupts you with in-app alerts.
  • You scroll logs to see what happened
    Each task has a status, a report and its proofWhat is happening now, what is finished, and the screenshots to show it.
  • Status is whatever an agent last wrote
    Kemiri works out each task's stateFrom task updates, blocking questions, live work, blockers, failures and deploys.
  • You check in to see if anything moved
    The coordinator waits, then wakesWorker reports wake it; regular check-ins help it spot work that may be stuck.
  • Anyone can call it done
    Review requests, not every partThe coordinator checks and closes finished parts. You review the request with its results and proof.
  • A restart loses the thread
    Runs resume after a normal restartReports and artifacts stay with their tasks, ready when you are.

While you're away

  1. A worker finishesIts final report reaches the coordinator once, even across a restart. New results wake it.
  2. 10 min check-insBy default, while workers run, the coordinator gets a progress digest to help spot a stuck worker.
  3. 15 min quietQuiet in-progress work becomes Paused after 15 minutes with nothing running; its coordinator gets one nudge per quiet spell.
  4. Blockers clearCleared task blockers notify the coordinator. A dated outside wait returns to review when its date arrives.
  5. NightlyWith a maintenance model configured, changed memory gets a nightly pass to merge duplicates and resolve contradictions.
  6. Back at the boardNeeds you gathers failures, blocking questions and reviews. Each task's Now line shows its current activity.

Start on your own machine

Three steps to your first task.

Pinned install: v0.1.0-pilot.6 (release notes). Linux tested, macOS and WSL experimental, no Android push in the public APK.

  1. Install and start

    Read the small bootstrap before you run it. It fetches the release installer and checks its SHA-256.

    Terminal
    curl -fL https://kemiri.dev/install.sh -o install.sh
# Read install.sh before running it.
sh install.sh --version v0.1.0-pilot.6 --no-service
kemiri start

  2. Open and pair

    In another terminal. Open the private pairing link in the same browser, and keep it private.

    Another terminal
    kemiri open
kemiri pair

  3. Set up once, then ask

    Connect a provider, choose a project folder and pick a coordinator model. Then say what you want, and step in when a task asks for you.

    Add dark mode and fix login on mobile.

Take the view with you

Download the signed Android APK (Android 12+). Your phone needs your backend reachable over HTTPS; the APK is not a tunnel.

You need git; install jj for isolated workers. Short install: curl -fsSL https://kemiri.dev/install.sh | sh. Checksums detect corruption, not a compromised publisher. Full guide and direct GitHub downloads.

Personal, not sandboxed. Kemiri runs commands with your permissions and provider spending authority. Every paired device has full owner access. This is not a multi-user service. Keep the backend private.
